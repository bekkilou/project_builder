Thank you for participating in our researc

This prototype is being tested to help us understand whether the questions we’re asking make sense and whether people feel able to provide appropriate answers.

What you’re doing in this prototype

You have already completed the filter questions and are now proceeding with an application journey.

This prototype focuses on core question sets that apply to all study types, regardless of complexity. You will see questions about:

  • study design
  • public involvement
  • risks and conflicts of interest
  • transparency and reporting

Some study types and requirements are not included yet, such as:

  • CTIMPs
  • research involving radiation
  • research involving children
  • other specialist or conditional sections

These will be added in later iterations of the service.

What you’ll need to complete the questions

To give realistic answers, it may help to have to hand the documents or information you would normally use when completing an application, for example:

  • a study protocol or outline
  • information about public or patient involvement
  • risk assessments or governance documentation
  • details on conflicts of interest and reporting plans

You do not need to create new content specifically for this test — we’re interested in how well the questions work with information you already have.

Saving your answers

This prototype does not save your progress.

For each section:

  • Answer the questions in that section
  • Download a CSV file of your responses
  • Complete a short survey about the questions you’ve just answered
  • Move on to the next section

What we want your feedback on

We know this service is incomplete. That’s expected.

We’re particularly interested in:

  • whether you understand what each question is asking
  • whether you feel able to give an appropriate answer
  • where wording or structure is unclear
  • whether anything important feels missing at this stage

Your survey responses are just as important as the answers you enter into the prototype.

If something doesn’t work

Because this is a prototype, some things may not behave as expected.

If you experience:

  • broken links
  • pages not loading
  • problems downloading your CSV

Please note this in the survey when prompted.

Need help taking part?

If you’re unsure how to continue, the best approach is to:

  • answer the questions as you would in a real application
  • make reasonable assumptions where information is missing
  • record any uncertainty or frustration in the survey

Please note this in the survey when prompted.

Thank you for taking part in this research and helping us improve the service.
