Configure your project scope

We will ask you a series of questions about your project to determine:

  • what approvals and permissions may be required
  • which review bodies may need to assess your project
  • what information you will need to provide as part of your application

Your answers will be used to generate a tailored approvals pathway for your project.

Before you start

It may help to have the following information ready:

  • the participant groups involved in your project
  • the activities your project will include, such as clinical procedures, use of data or biological samples
  • whether the project involves NHS organisations, prisoners, Ministry of Defence sites, or other regulated settings

You will need to answer all questions to generate your approvals pathway. You can save your progress and return later if needed.
