Design variant A
The approval landscape
Spatial overview shown before the application begins. Applicants see the whole territory — what is always required, what they may trigger, and what automatically disqualifies Proportionate Review. Suited to new applicants who need orientation. Not interactive beyond hover.

Your application will need to go through a Research Ethics Committee. Depending on your answers, it may also need approval from one or more additional bodies. Some answers will prevent you from using the faster Proportionate Review route.

Always required
Research Ethics Committee (REC) review
HRA / HCRW assessment
Triggered by your answers
MHRA
If your project is a CTIMP, or a clinical investigation of a medical device, you will need to apply to the Medicines and Healthcare products Regulatory Agency.
CTIMP = never Proportionate Review
ARSAC
If your project exposes participants to ionising radiation — particularly diagnostic or therapeutic administration of radioactive substances — you will need Administration of Radioactive Substances Advisory Committee approval.
CAG / Section 251
If you will access identifiable patient data without consent and without an existing legal basis, you may need approval from the Confidentiality Advisory Group.
HMPPS / NIPS / SPS RAEC
If your project includes prisoners or young offenders in custody, the relevant prison service authority for that UK nation must approve your research.
Prisoners = never Proportionate Review
HFEA
If your project involves HFEA-regulated activities (embryo storage, gamete use to create embryos) or accesses the HFEA Register, you will need a Human Fertilisation and Embryology Authority licence or approval.
HTA licence
If your project involves collection or use of human tissue, your institution will need a relevant Human Tissue Authority licence. You will be asked to provide licence details in your application.
MODREC (Ministry of Defence)
If your project involves MOD-regulated activity, IRAS will signpost you to the MOD's own review process. This is not managed within IRAS.
External to IRAS workflow
Answers that prevent Proportionate Review
Your project is a CTIMP (clinical trial of an investigational medicinal product)
You are establishing a research bioresource
You are using medical devices that are not marketed in the EU or UK
Adults lacking capacity will be included as participants
Consent will not be obtained and the reason includes adults lacking capacity
Your project includes prisoners or young offenders in custody
Certain NHS care setting participant types (nursing homes in Northern Ireland, other social care settings)
