Transparency

Being transparent about your research builds trust.

This section contains questions about the ways you will ensure your project is carried out openly and transparently.

We’ll ask you about:

  • research registration details
  • deferral requests
  • contact details for the research that will be made publicly available
  • plans for sharing of samples or data beyond this research project

All questions are mandatory unless marked as optional.

You don’t have to answer all these questions in one session – you can save and come back later if you need to.
